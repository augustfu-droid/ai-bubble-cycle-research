## Disclaimer

This report is independent research prepared by Daduizhang from public information. It does not represent any organization with which the author is affiliated and is not personalized investment advice, a guarantee of returns, or an instruction to trade. The research judgments may be wrong, and public disclosures may subsequently be revised.

The research cutoff is 31 October 2026, Beijing time. Financial, market, technical and policy data retain the reporting periods or observation dates stated in the text; they are not all contemporaneous values. Company guidance, conditional illustrations and realized results are distinguished. Unsupported inputs are left open, and scenarios that have not been calibrated are not assigned precise probabilities.

The text is organized around current assessments rather than historical pages with rolling annotations. Earlier drafts are archived separately, and the conditions attached to earlier forecasts have not been rewritten. Verification limits are stated with the relevant sources; publication of this edition does not constitute a fresh audit of every historical fact.

5. Carried forward the verified V1.8 financial baseline for cloud providers and the supply chain. New video-model operating evidence is added, with group revenue, product revenue and microdrama attribution treated separately.

6. Distinguished contractual cash obligations, cancellable ceilings, leases and guarantees by their nature. Single-source information and verification gaps are identified separately.

7. Retained the V1.8 baseline for Chinese supply-chain cash flows and the forward calendar. The coding chapter constructs separate Chinese and US labor-cost scenarios rather than applying US wages directly to China.

8. Retained the existing evidence on models, independent tests, energy and international constraints. The deeper chapters examine the real economic effects of video generation and software agents.

9. Added assessments of how microdrama production demand and software labor automation propagate through the industry. The 31 October macroeconomic baseline is retained; new case counts are not converted into crash probabilities.

10. Retained conditional risk scenarios and updated the relevant financial inputs and financial conditions. Historical instrument and legal baselines do not become newly verified merely because the edition date changes.

11. Reorganized the factual checks around current arguments, listing carried-forward baselines and unresolved gaps. Historical audits and forecast scoring are traced through their separate archives.

12. Rebuilt the contents, bookmarks and page numbers from this edition's text. Changes are listed individually, sources are collected in one reference index, and the pale-blue charts and author watermark are retained.
