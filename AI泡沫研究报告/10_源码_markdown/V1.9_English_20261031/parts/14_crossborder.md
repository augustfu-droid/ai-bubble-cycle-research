## Appendix B: Cross-Border Channels and Instrument Boundaries

A judgment about the AI capital cycle cannot be converted directly into a personal trading list. Even if an investor correctly judges the direction of earnings and valuations, losses may still result from an inapplicable funding channel, mismatched contract maturity, a narrowing premium, inadequate margin or differences in product structure. This chapter retains V1.7's explanation of instrument mechanics. The legal-information verification date remains October 27, 2026; neither V1.8 nor this V1.9 reverified every jurisdiction, broker rule and product contract. Specific applicability is subject to the rules and contracts in force at the relevant time, and this chapter does not replace licensed institutions' reviews of identity, source of funds and product suitability.

### I. Channels Before Products: Four Arrangements Are Not One Offshore Account

Any discussion of cross-border investment must answer at least four separate questions: who may participate, how funds enter and exit, what assets may be held, and who bears distribution and custody responsibilities. A product's listing in Hong Kong or the United States answers only the listing-venue question. It does not establish that an individual in mainland China can buy it through any account, still less that the purpose of personal foreign-exchange purchases has been authorized.

| Arrangement | Relationship between funds and assets | Boundary this chapter can establish |
|---|---|---|
| Southbound Cross-boundary Wealth Management Connect | Eligible participants invest in eligible products through paired dedicated accounts, with a closed-loop flow of funds | Banks and securities firms have their respective participation and distribution arrangements; this is not a general Hong Kong/US stock account |
| Southbound Stock Connect | Trading within the mutual market-access mechanism and the applicable eligible list | Listing on HKEX does not equal inclusion in Southbound Stock Connect; ETFs also have specific eligibility requirements |
| QDII funds and similar products | Qualified domestic institutions raise, custody and manage offshore investment portfolios | Individuals hold fund units; this does not confer eligibility to remit funds independently to any offshore account |
| General offshore securities accounts and services | Also involves the account-opening jurisdiction, location of service provision, client identity and source of funds | The existence of an account, access to software or full offshore licensing is not independently sufficient to establish that the relevant services offered into mainland China and funding route are compliant |

The rectification arrangements for illegal cross-border securities, futures and fund operations announced on May 22, 2026 target offshore institutions and assisting parties conducting relevant business in mainland China without approval, and include requirements to clear illegal existing business. Their scope cannot be expanded to mean all offshore accounts receive identical treatment, nor can existing accounts be assumed automatically exempt. The CSRC's same-day Q&A explicitly listed Southbound Stock Connect, QDII and Cross-boundary Wealth Management Connect as lawful channels. Whether a particular account is affected by a measure still requires checking the facts of the institution, service and funds. [CSRC, Q&A on the rectification plan, 2026-05-22](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7634328/content.shtml)

#### Wealth Management Connect: Regional Eligibility, a Closed Loop and a Product Pool All Apply

The HKMA's bank Southbound Scheme page was revised on May 11, 2026: eligibility includes a Greater Bay Area identity or residency connection and mainland regulatory requirements, and dedicated accounts operate under closed-loop management. Products include qualifying funds, bonds and deposits, but exclude investment products listed and traded on HKEX. The individual net-remittance quota is RMB 3 million, allocated as RMB 1.5 million each when both bank and securities-firm channels are used. This is a fund-flow quota, not an asset-market-value ceiling or an appropriate risk budget. [HKMA, bank Southbound Scheme arrangements, revised 2026-05-11](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/key-functions/international-financial-centre/wealth-management-connect/southbound-scheme/)

Participation by securities firms also does not mean a dedicated account becomes an unrestricted Hong Kong stock-trading account. The SFC topic page rechecked in this review was updated on January 22, 2026 and lists the foundational circular dated January 24, 2024 and supplementary arrangements dated November 13, 2025. Paragraphs 35–36 of the original appendix specify the scope of funds and bonds and exclude products listed and traded on HKEX. The 2025 supplement primarily improves customer communications and product-introduction arrangements; it did not provide a basis for finding that general stock trading had been opened. [SFC, Wealth Management Connect topic page](https://www.sfc.hk/en/Regulatory-functions/Intermediaries/Supervision/Cross-boundary-WMC); [Appendix 1 to the 2024 circular](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/api/circular/openAppendix?appendix=0&lang=EN&refNo=24EC6); [2025 supplementary circular](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/api/circular/list-content/circular/intermediaries/supervision/doc?lang=EN&refNo=25EC60)

Document dates are retained here to distinguish a current webpage check from the original issuance date of a rule. This chapter has not verified any individual's eligibility or any institution's entire saleable-product list, nor does it describe the Hong Kong materials above as a compilation of all 2026 implementing rules in mainland China, Hong Kong and Macao. Product pools, institutional arrangements and suitability outcomes still require confirmation against the full documents at the time services are actually provided.

#### Southbound Stock Connect and QDII: Having a Channel Does Not Mean Having Every Instrument

HKEX's March 2026 ETF Connect materials state that eligible ETFs exclude synthetic ETFs and leveraged and inverse products. Thus, "a particular inverse product exists in Hong Kong" and "that product can be bought through Southbound Stock Connect" are two different statements. This chapter has not verified every entry on the day's eligible list, and does not equate similar index names with identical product eligibility. [HKEX, ETF Connect information for issuers, 2026-03](https://www.hkex.com.hk/-/media/HKEX-Market/Products/Securities/Exchange-Traded-Products/Launch/Inclusion-of-ETFs-in-Stock-Connect-info-for-issuers.pdf)

QDII, by contrast, refers to institutions approved to raise funds domestically and manage offshore securities investments as portfolios; fundholders obtain rights under the relevant product contract. Investment scope, use of derivatives, subscription and redemption arrangements, and unit-trading methods still need contract-by-contract review. The label "QDII" does not imply uniform risk or tax treatment. [CSRC, Articles 2 and 3 of the Trial Measures for QDII Administration, issued in 2007](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c106256/c1653919/content.shtml)

#### Foreign Exchange, Accounts and Tax Cannot Substitute for One Another

SAFE's business materials published in 2023 explain that the equivalent of USD 50,000 is an individual's annual facilitation quota, not an absolute monetary ceiling on all lawful foreign-exchange uses, and not automatic permission for offshore securities investment. Foreign-exchange purchases under the facilitation arrangement have purpose restrictions. This review retains that basic boundary without presenting the 2023 materials as new 2026 rules, and makes no legal conclusion about a particular individual's full capital-account eligibility. [State Administration of Foreign Exchange, personal foreign-exchange business explanation, page 16 of the 2023 materials](https://www.safe.gov.cn/shanxi/file/file/20230704/261d099ca05b479c82f28d0b5e16cd57.pdf)

Analytically, compliance in account opening, cross-border service provision and remittance purpose, and tax obligations are related checks that cannot substitute for one another. Filing taxes does not automatically remedy an inapplicable funding route, and owning offshore assets does not automatically determine a single tax rate. Because this research lacks information on personal tax residence, asset location, income character and tax credits, it provides no uniform after-tax return or personal filing judgment.

### II. QDII Prices Contain Two Layers of Risk: NAV Changes and Discount/Premium Changes

When a fund holding offshore assets trades on a domestic secondary market, its price does not simply follow the offshore index. Fund NAV incorporates the portfolio, fees, exchange rates and other factors, while the secondary-market price is additionally affected by subscription/redemption access, trading hours and supply and demand. China Southern Asset Management's S&P 500 ETF premium-risk notice on September 24, 2026 explicitly warned that the secondary-market price was significantly above indicative NAV and that trading suspensions might be used as warnings. This was an issuer disclosure at that time, not a real-time premium quote on this report's evidence-collection date. [China Southern Asset Management, secondary-market trading-price premium-risk notice, 2026-09-24](https://epaper.cs.com.cn/zgzqb/html/2026-09/24/nw.D110000zgzqb_20260924_3-B062.htm)

Writing a unit price as "NAV at a comparable time × (1 + premium rate)" gives the following under simplified assumptions excluding distributions, taxes, fees and transaction costs:

Price return＝(1＋NAV return)×(1＋ending premium rate)÷(1＋initial premium rate)−1.

Assume NAV falls from 100 to 50, the purchase premium is 30%, and the sale discount is 5%. The price then falls from 130 to 47.5, producing a price return of −63.46%, rather than simply adding −50%, −30% and −5%. This example illustrates structure only; it does not mean any particular fund will experience these changes. Conversely, a rising NAV may be offset by a narrowing premium, while a falling underlying market may temporarily be obscured by a widening premium.

Valuation times must also be aligned in research: dividing an intraday domestic price by stale NAV from the previous offshore trading day may produce a "premium" containing market and exchange-rate changes not yet reflected in the accounts. Indicative NAV also does not mean every holder can immediately redeem at that figure. If the NAV used already includes exchange-rate effects, the model must not deduct RMB appreciation or depreciation again. Restricted subscriptions may weaken arbitrage, but guarantee neither a widening premium nor its reversion on a particular trading day.

Research records should therefore retain separately the underlying-asset date, NAV date, domestic quote time, subscription/redemption status and supporting announcements. Without simultaneous data, the premium remains "to be verified"; an apparently cheap price label is not upgraded into an executable trading condition.

### III. Being Right on Direction Can Still Lose Money: Additional Variables in Five Instrument Types

| Instrument type | Additional requirements beyond the research judgment | Inference that cannot be made |
|---|---|---|
| Buying put options | Size and timing of the decline, implied volatility, spreads, exercise and settlement arrangements | A correct long-term bearish view means a finite-maturity contract must profit |
| Shorting stock | Continued stock-borrow availability, borrow fees, margin, dividend compensation and forced-closeout conditions | A high company valuation gives losses a natural ceiling |
| Index futures | Contract multiplier, mark-to-market cash, margin changes, basis and rolling | Initial margin is the maximum possible loss |
| Leveraged and inverse products | Daily objectives, reset path, expenses, trading and capacity constraints | Multi-month returns must equal the index's cumulative return multiplied by the multiple in the product name |
| Volatility-related products | Actual tracked underlying, term structure, rolling, issuer and termination terms | A rise in spot VIX makes every related product rise in the same proportion |

A put option compresses "the price may fall" into a stricter proposition: the price must fall within the contract's life and by enough to cover the premium and costs. The premium on a separately purchased option may be lost entirely; even if the underlying falls, the option may lose money because implied volatility falls or time passes. Expiry and exercise may also create new settlement obligations or underlying positions, so "a purchased option has limited loss" cannot be extended indefinitely to the entire account after exercise. [SEC, options investor education, updated 2026-07-16](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-63)

Shorting stock and buying a put have different risk structures. A short position's price loss theoretically has no fixed ceiling and also involves borrow costs, dividend compensation and margin requirements. Long-term overvaluation can coexist with further short-term gains. Futures margin is performance collateral, not the full purchase price; daily marking to market converts price moves into cash requirements. Being "ultimately right on direction" cannot replace the ability to meet payments along the way. [SEC, basic short-selling risk explanation](https://www.sec.gov/investor/pubs/regsho.htm); [CFTC, futures margin and marking to market](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/EducationCenter/economicpurpose)

No specific broker fees or margin figures are listed here because they vary with the contract, client, volatility and institutional policies, and no account-level verification was conducted in this review. More importantly, research scenarios typically operate over longer horizons than instruments' funding and risk-management cycles. Expressing a long-term view with a tool requiring continual cash replenishment introduces another question the original research did not answer.

#### Daily Inverse Does Not Mean Inverse Over the Whole Period

Ignoring expenses, tracking error and rebalancing constraints, if an underlying rises 10% on the first day and falls 9.0909% on the second, it goes from 100 back to 100. An ideal daily −2x product instead falls from 100 to 80 and then rises to approximately 94.55, returning approximately −5.45% over the two days. This comes from the daily compounding path; it cannot be calculated by multiplying the underlying's two-day total return of 0 by −2.

This example also does not prove that a product decays at a fixed rate every day: persistent one-way trends, oscillation and the order of gains and losses can produce different results. There is no universal "annual decay of 15%–30%" for all inverse products, still less a long-term insurance cost that can be inferred from structural education.

Flexible-leverage structures appearing in 2026 add another constraint. Hong Kong's Investor and Financial Education Council explained on August 3 that some products' target multiples may be lowered daily and are not fixed each day at the upper limit shown in their names; the next day's target must be disclosed by the issuer. This research therefore does not carry forward a fixed-multiple list as representative of all current products, nor conflate HKEX listing eligibility with Southbound Stock Connect eligibility. [Hong Kong IFEC, flexible leverage structure, 2026-08-03](https://www.ifec.org.hk/web/en/investment/investment-products/leveraged-and-inverse-products/flexible-leverage-structure.page)

#### Volatility Exposure and Downside Insurance Are Not Synonyms

Many volatility exchange-traded products track VIX futures rather than spot VIX, and term structure and rolling affect holding results; ETNs also require checking issuer credit and other terms. Inferring "holding for a year must lose 60%–90%" from "prolonged market calm hurts some products," or "any VIX product provides long-term insurance" from "volatility rises during stock-market panic," goes beyond product-specific evidence. [FINRA, risks of volatility-linked exchange-traded products, 2017-10-26](https://syndication.finra.org/content/know-you-invest-volatility-linked-exchange-traded-products)

Common non-principal-protected snowball products tie coupons to knock-in, knock-out and other barrier conditions; economically, the buyer may resemble a seller of an exotic put, taking on rather than removing some downside risk. Different structures may include limited losses, protection clauses or different observation arrangements, so generalization is inappropriate. The coupon is not deposit interest, still less insurance that automatically pays out when AI assets fall. [CSRC Guangdong bureau, key trading elements of snowball products, 2021-08-24](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/guangdong/c105589/c3fc6e55bf3e64605959a103db73ad5b0/content.shtml)

### IV. Cross-Asset Buffers Must Be Judged by the Source of the Shock

"Technology stocks fall" is insufficient to establish the direction of gold, long-duration bonds, the US dollar or A-shares. If the shock comes from rising real interest rates, discounting pressure on duration assets may increase simultaneously; if it comes from falling demand with easing inflation constraints, the rate path may differ; if it comes from a liquidity squeeze, assets previously treated as buffers may also be sold. These are conditional relationships requiring separate estimation, not fixed correlations, still less a permanent hedging table.

Gold provides no contractual payment opposite to AI-company cash flow; bond prices depend on duration, credit and interest rates; foreign-currency assets also connect investment outcomes to domestic-currency liabilities. Chinese assets differ from US technology companies in business, policy and valuation foundations, but may still interact through exports, supply chains, risk appetite and global funding channels. Industry demand, share prices and exchange-rate changes require separate arguments. "Different regions" does not imply "no transmission."

For a portfolio, the real questions are which risk factor causes the original loss, whether a new instrument reduces that factor, and whether it instead adds new maturity, leverage, credit or liquidity risk. Without all holdings and liabilities, a purported net hedge effect cannot be calculated. This chapter therefore does not convert conditional analysis into personal gold, cash or cross-market allocation percentages.

### V. A Further Gate Remains Between Research and Implementation

Before a research view enters an actual decision, at least five mutually consistent records are needed: the applicable channel and identity basis; the specific product's latest contract and risk documents; funding arrangements matched to liability currencies; profit/loss and cash requirements including the worst path; and evidence conditions that would overturn the original judgment. These are not a purchase list, but boundaries preventing "understanding the story" from being mistaken for "having an executable plan."

This verification established that the channels are not interchangeable, identified structural risks in several products, and confirmed the dates of relevant official documents. It did not establish any individual's eligibility to open accounts, buy foreign exchange or trade, or their suitability; did not reread live prices, full product lists, fees or tax treatment; and executed no trades. Industry-risk research remains research at this point. Only additional complete facts and explicit authorization could support a separate discussion of implementation.
