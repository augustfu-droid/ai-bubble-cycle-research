## Research Overview: Growth Continues, but Cash Recovery Is Diverging

The information cutoff is 31 October 2026 (Beijing time); market interest-rate observations run through 30 October in the United States. V1.9 retains the financial, macroeconomic and other-application baselines from V1.8, with deeper analysis of how microdramas affect video-model providers, comparisons of time spent, and the effects of AI coding on labor, hiring and actual costs. Reporting periods, announcement dates and evidence-collection dates remain separate; a new version does not turn undisclosed or unobtained items into current observations.

The current evidence supports three levels of judgment: operating growth in cloud services and parts of the supply chain continues to materialize; several companies continue to spend more on construction than their operating cash flow in the same period; and new applications are expanding the range of tasks for which AI can charge, without demonstrating adequate returns on all additional infrastructure. This edition therefore places greater emphasis on cash coverage and new firm payment obligations, while retaining counterevidence from expanding demand, project delivery and falling unit costs. It does not confirm that financing and demand have both collapsed across the industry.

### Why New Applications Require Separate Research

AI-video microdramas (short-form episodic dramas) deserve inclusion in the commercialization framework because audiences can pay for content or subscriptions, and advertisers can pay for viewers' attention. These revenue sources are easier to trace than a general claim of “improved model capability.” But the overall live-action microdrama market, AI-assisted production, primarily AI-generated microdramas and model-tool revenue are four different measures. The new analysis compares distribution, payment, customer acquisition, IP and copyright in China and the United States, and uses conditional illustrations to test who captures the savings from cheaper production and whether paid user acquisition, rework and competitive price cuts consume them. Market forecasts, platform self-reports and realized industry revenue remain separate; total microdrama revenue does not substitute for AI revenue.

The new evidence extends beyond view counts: Duiba separately disclosed RMB 222.629 million in AI-microdrama revenue for 2026H1, but the group still lost RMB 25.326 million in that period, so a standalone net margin for its AI business cannot be inferred. COL disclosed cost reductions from switching some overseas microdramas to AI production, although its cumulative and single-quarter cash trends differ. This increases the importance of fees actually charged, while making a complete accounting of unsuccessful titles, customer acquisition and collection cycles more necessary. See Chapter 4 for specific reporting periods, original documents and calculations. [Duiba interim report](https://yun.duiba.com.cn/corp/report/content/1/2026%E4%B8%AD%E6%9C%9F%E5%A0%B1%E5%91%8A.3f87c290d81d43e1b87da58f7eaf9818.pdf), [COL third-quarter report](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-10-26/1225620142.PDF)

Video models themselves already generate revenue: Kling exceeded RMB 650 million and RMB 850 million in the two quarters of the first half, respectively; MiniMax's first-half group revenue was USD 116.573 million, with gross profit of USD 20.813 million and R&D of USD 296.870 million. The former does not disclose a complete video-profit breakdown; the latter derives 63.4% from its open platform, which mixes multiple modalities. Neither can be attributed entirely to microdramas. Seedance's external API and ByteDance's internal usage, and the Hailuo creative product and H3 open weights, also require separate accounting. Microdramas can generate professional production orders, but model customers may buy fewer unusable clips as acceptance yields improve. Prices, net paid output, inference costs and R&D jointly determine upstream returns. [Kuaishou Q1](https://ir.kuaishou.com/node/11461/pdf), [Kuaishou Q2](https://ir.kuaishou.com/zh-hans/news-releases/news-release-details/kuaishoukejifabu2026niandierjidujizhongqiweijingshenhecaiwuyeji), [MiniMax interim results](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0826/2026082600680.pdf)

The claim that “microdrama viewing time exceeds movies” must be narrowed to what the sources support: QuestMobile's 125 minutes for Hongguo in February 2026 measures “daily app usage time per monthly user”; Omdia's 35.7 minutes for ReelShort versus 24.8 minutes for Netflix in the United States refers to each mobile app's own users in 2025Q4. Comparing the former with a movie's running time is merely an analogy; the latter excludes television, computers and cinemas and is not a comparison of total viewing. These figures support high usage intensity, but do not establish displacement of movies across the market; video generation also cannot be counted repeatedly by multiplying it by the number of viewers. [QuestMobile original](https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2041710682848727041/), [Omdia original](https://omdia.tech.informa.com/pr/2026/feb/microdramas-overtake-streamers-on-mobile-engagement-says-omdia)

### How the Coding Review and Other Applications Change the Assessment

Earlier coding estimates cannot all be classified as “correct forecasts.” 36.50 million professional developers × USD 30 × 12 gives 13.14B: conditional arithmetic. Usage-based pricing, tiered plans and nonprofessional uses have invalidated the assumption that fixed low-price seats cover all revenue. The early original draft also contradicted its own high-price scenario with an “absolute ceiling” and contained a $131B unit-scale error; V1.5 withdrew the hard ceiling, and this review uses historical originals to complete the item-by-item judgments. Public product metrics still do not establish that the deduplicated pure-coding market has exceeded 13.14B; correcting a model cannot substitute for new realized market results. See Chapter 5 for the evidence and future validation windows.

Evidence of labor substitution must also move beyond “more code” to organizations. The revised Chen–Stratton study of 655 firms finds that gains at the generation stage weaken as they pass through to final delivery, and detects no significant overall employment change. Stanford's ADP study, meanwhile, finds a narrower entry point into employment for younger workers in highly exposed occupations. These findings do not negate one another: fewer tasks can first appear as fewer hires and less outsourcing. The latter's 19% relative difference in employment trajectories does not mean AI laid off 19% of programmers, and is certainly not a displacement rate for China. [Original organizational study](https://fion.ac/jellyfish.pdf), [ADP study](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/app/uploads/2026/08/Canaries_August2026.pdf)

Chapter 5 provides two complete sets of author assumptions. All benefits require equivalent delivery and usable capacity; risk budgets are listed separately, and the results cannot be treated as industry averages:

| Steady-state illustration | Released labor and cost value | All staff retained, no additional cash collected | Alternative path where costs can actually be removed |
|---|---|---|---|
| US team of 10 | Net release of 2400 hours, labor-cost value USD 240,000; after USD 40,000 of incremental spending and risk budget, net capacity value is USD 200,000 | Cash spending and risk budget increase by USD 40,000 | If a full-year hire costing USD 180,000 is genuinely avoided, net benefit is USD 140,000 |
| Chinese team of 8 | Net release of 900 hours, labor-cost value RMB 120,000; deducting RMB 90,000 leaves net capacity value of RMB 30,000 | Cash spending and risk budget increase by RMB 90,000 | If 900 hours of outsourcing at RMB 220/hour can be cancelled and absorbed by this capacity, net benefit is RMB 108,000 |

The first year also requires training, integration and parallel-validation costs, so steady-state benefits cannot be applied directly. Internal capacity valuation, avoided hires and outsourcing savings cannot be added together if they overlap. What expands is the range of chargeable tasks and customer budgets, not a direct conversion of the global wage pool into model revenue. Equivalent quality, actual cash realization and suppliers' profits after inference costs are what require continuing validation.

Other applications retain the earlier report's fourteen-domain framework and evidence verified in V1.8: AlphaSense's company ARR exceeded 0.7B in Q2; Duolingo's complete product generates positive cash flow, which cannot all be attributed to AI features; Character.ai entered a court-supervised sale process, with service continuing at the time. Alibaba's mixed models-and-applications segment, Tempus and autonomous driving likewise require separation of revenue, nonoperating gains and operating losses. Chapter 6 does not aggregate these heterogeneous metrics; it examines payers, full costs and recovery case by case. [AlphaSense original disclosure](https://www.alpha-sense.com/press/alphasense-named-the-only-leader-in-market-and-competitive-intelligence-platforms/), [Character.ai official status](https://blog.character.ai/an-update-on-character-ai/).

### Financial Conditions and Subsequent Verification

On 28 October, the Federal Reserve kept its policy rate at 3.75%—4.00%; on 30 October, nominal and real ten-year Treasury yields were 4.94% and 2.60%, respectively, both down 6 basis points from 26 October. September PCE inflation of +3.6% year on year and core inflation of +3.1% still require attention to inflation. An unchanged policy rate can coexist with falling Treasury yields, while corporate credit costs may differ again. A new crash probability cannot be inferred directly from one macroeconomic variable.
